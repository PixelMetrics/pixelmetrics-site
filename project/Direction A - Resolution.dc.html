Resolution

Detail goes exactly where the work is, and nowhere else.

A quadtree is how computers decide where detail belongs: split a square in four, but only where there is information. PixelMetrics works the same way, it doesn’t redesign a whole business, it resolves the parts that leak time.

1px · the drain we fix

Pixel + Metrics. The name already holds the idea: the pixel as a unit of measure. Every surface in this identity is measured in squares that halve, 1, 4, 16, 64, until they reach a single pixel.

Why it’s theirs. Operators don’t add detail everywhere; they zoom into the one step that wastes hours. “We find the drain” becomes a visual law: resolution is spent only where it matters, and the deepest cell is always marked in vermilion.

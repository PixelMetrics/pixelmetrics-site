Channels

Look closely at any pixel: it’s three. Step back: it’s one.

Every pixel on a screen is made of three subpixels working as one. PixelMetrics is three disciplines, software, websites, automation, delivered by one accountable team. The identity is built from that structure: three channels, in register.
01 Central idea
From across the room 1 pixel · one team
Softwareapps · tools · integrations
Websitesdesign · build · launch
Automationagents · workflows · ops

Why it’s theirs. Most agencies sell one of these and subcontract the rest. PixelMetrics’ entire pitch is that the three are inseparable, a website that doesn’t connect to the software, or automation that isn’t run by the people who built it, is a broken pixel.

Measurable. Every project has a signature, how much of each channel it took. The “metrics” in the name becomes a visible, honest record of what was built.
